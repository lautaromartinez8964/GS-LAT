# Prediction workflow

1. Build a histogram in the supplied SAR values. Use an eligible interpeak valley or the dark-side triangle cut. If the triangle cut degenerates to the first bin, rebuild the histogram with the 99th-percentile upper bound and use the eligible valley or longest-tail triangle cut. The seed is used for geometry and the initial prior.
2. Build the D3E5 core (3 x 3 dilation, then 5 x 5 erosion), extract Canny edges, and use a shared edge buffer and tight bounding box for geometry and support. Split oversized sources. Measure short-axis profiles with a transverse strip width derived from 1/16 of the short side, or use normal fallback profiles. Keep ordered profiles with core median < threshold < bank median. Require at least two ordered profiles per source, apply the 0.15 scene-rank contrast gate, and prune nested boxes.
3. Combine source thresholds into an anchor field `M` and support strength `q`. The accepted profiles also supply dark-core and outer-bank sample values. Each side must contain at least eight finite samples. Normalize each profile's two class histograms separately, balance profiles within a source, then weight sources by reliability, rank contrast and bilateral/single-sided evidence.
4. Form up to 256 quantile bins in the original values, collapsing repeated edges. Smooth the class masses along the bin axis, add a small common pseudocount based on the scene histogram, and project the dark-class share of the two masses onto a nonincreasing sequence. No parametric distribution family is fitted.
5. Start with the image-wide seed fraction clipped to [0.01, 0.99]. Compute pixelwise posteriors once and average them over valid pixels in a neighborhood whose radius comes from the profiles. Clip the average to [0.01, 0.99] and use it directly as the local prior `pi`, without shrinking it toward the initial prior. Obtain `T_stat` from a lookup of minimum equal-cost classification risk at the bin boundaries.
6. Use the support strength directly as the geometric weight, `w = q`. The final threshold is `T = q * M + (1 - q) * T_stat`. Thus `q = 0` leaves the statistical threshold in charge. No entropy factor and no dark-mean floor are applied. Classify valid values at or below `T`, replace isolated pixels synchronously, and remove 8-connected foreground components containing at most four pixels.
